Keep one ticket per supported cause. Decision 0002 supersedes decision 0001. A subject match finds a candidate ticket; it does not authorize a merge.

Before merging

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID, so overlapping pages do not inflate the count.
  2. Read the body. Record the intended local date, actual date and affected action. Check whether it describes month/day reversal, a one-day shift or event ordering.
  3. Resolve the tenant's configured locale and booking form. Browser locale and intake tags can disagree with this configuration.
  4. Check the processing path. For day-first tenants, booking-v3 form reports are hint candidates; app/email and booking-v2 form reports are parser candidates under the exported model.
  5. Compare the evidence with the candidate ticket. Merge only when symptoms and a supported common cause agree. If the cause or tenant is unknown, keep the report separate and collect evidence.

Timing exceptions

Check desk-notes.json and the current incident analysis. Its timing exceptions remain provisional even though the original classifier calls them parser. Collect original timestamps, offsets, intended local dates, calendar payloads and delivery logs. Do not assume a parser fix, a timezone cause or a shared cause among the exceptions.
